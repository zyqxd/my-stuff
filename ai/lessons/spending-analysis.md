# Lessons — spending analysis

- Present budget recommendations in four broad decision buckets by default; offer detailed subcategories only on request.
- When David says “try again” after a stalled analysis, resume with executed evidence and a decisive result; do not repeat setup or prior reasoning. Applies to spendr investigations; repeated on 2026-08-10.
- A linked refund must inherit its purchase category; never permit a confirmed link with a null category. Reject new links to uncategorized purchases, cascade later purchase-category changes for legacy repair, and derive Review eligibility from the missing category rather than status alone.
- Income is a cash-flow flow, not a spending category: leave its category null, hide it from the default spending ledger, and exclude it from every spending insight. Add an explicit ignored flow for exceptional one-sided rows; ignored rows remain visible under “show everything” but count in neither cash flow nor insights.
